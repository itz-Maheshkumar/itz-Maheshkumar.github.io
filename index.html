I'm an MSc Computer Science student who spends most of my time building LLM applications on open-source models and poking at agentic AI problems — systems that plan, use tools, and chain steps together on their own.

I study AI at a full-stack level: from the model and infra layer up through the application layer, because understanding how a thing works underneath makes me better at building with it.
